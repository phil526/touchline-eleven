Use V (Roll), B (Feint) or N (Fake) while carrying the ball in open play; aim to either side with movement. Each move needs a short recovery and can still be tackled. A fake shot cancels your current shot charge. For opponent penalties, choose a screen side with A/D or the stick, use W/S for centre, then press Space to commit to one timed dive. Assisted penalty saves are available in Settings. Practice → Penalty saves repeats kicks from the opponent. Tap Shoot just before an incoming ground pass reaches you to strike first time. The input waits up to 0.4 seconds for contact. Use R, LB + A, or hold Control and tap Pass for a one-two: the passer runs forward and you control the receiver. Aim towards the runner and press Through or Pass to return the ball. The run lasts up to five seconds and tries to stay onside until release. Close-range shooting assist can be switched off in Settings. Hold J / A / Pass or K / Y / Through to build passing power, then release. A quick tap plays a short pass. Move towards a passing option to aim; the gold diamond and dotted line show your receiver. Hold Ctrl, LT / L2 or Control for tighter dribbling at 80% speed. Near a defender, your player turns to shield the ball; SHIELDING shows when active. Release to resume normal dribbling. Hold Shoot to build power; the aiming guide shows your intended target. Move left/right to change aim, then release. Hold Z (RB / R1) with Shoot for a slower, more accurate curling shot. Hold X (LT / L2) with Shoot for a chip over an advancing keeper. On touch, choose Normal, Finesse or Chip before charging. Chip takes priority if both modifiers are held. The flight guide previews curve and height; accuracy still depends on pressure and shooting ability. Press F or Slide for a committed sliding challenge; contact with an opponent before the ball can concede a foul. Goal replays can be skipped with the button, Space, Esc, A or B. The triangle marks your player; the smaller ring marks a passing option. Standard controller mapping; press a button after connecting.
Free kicks, throw-ins, goal kicks, corners and penalties: release any held action after the whistle, then use movement or the stick to aim. Hold and release Pass or Shoot; full power waits for release. Loft sends a cross. For throw-ins, Pass makes a shorter throw and Shoot makes a long throw. Penalties automatically switch behind the taker. Move the target left/right and up/down with WASD, arrows or the movement stick, then hold and release Shoot. Your target stays where you leave it; power changes shot speed. Practice → Penalty Practice lets you repeat kicks against the keeper. Your team waits for your input, even at full power.
