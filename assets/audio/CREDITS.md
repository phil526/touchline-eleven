# Crowd recording attribution

Stadium ambience and cheering excerpts from **Free Crowd Cheering Sounds** by **Gregor Quendel**.

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/free-crowd-cheering-sounds
- Creator: https://www.gregorquendel.com/
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Source files: 10 (Ambience), 05 (Soft cheering I), 04 (Strong cheering II — Short).
- Changes: excerpts, filtering, level adjustment, crossfades for ambience loops, fades for one-shot reactions, resampled to 32 kHz stereo and encoded as 96 kbps MP3.

These recordings remain CC BY 4.0; the game's MIT licence does not replace their licence. Attribution is also available in the in-game Settings menu.
