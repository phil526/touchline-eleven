# Controls and accessibility

In Broadcast and Telebroadcast, screen-right is pitch +x and screen-up is pitch −z; the camera stays on the same touchline when teams change ends. Third person follows behind and above the selected player, keeping the avatar visible. Movement follows your view: forward moves where you look, left/right strafe. Drag on an empty part of the pitch (mouse or touch), or use the controller right stick, to orbit the player. The camera starts facing the attacking goal and changes ends at half-time. Switching players moves the follow camera to the new selection. Northstar attacks right in half one, Ember left. Move towards a teammate to favour that passing direction; no direction favours forward options. Shoot charges to a cap and releases, with forward-goal assistance. A full charge also automatically fires to prevent an indefinitely held shot.

The selected player has a ring and an overhead triangle. An aimed pass option has a gold diamond and a dotted ground line; a candidate for switching has a smaller faint diamond. Radar symbols differ in both shape and colour: round Northstar and square Ember. Player names, numbered shirts and HUD team labels give additional cues. While you own the ball, hold Ctrl, LT / L2 or the touch Control button for tighter dribbling at 80% of normal speed and quicker turns. Near a defender, Close Control turns your body to keep the ball on the far side; SHIELDING appears in your player card and on the touch button. You can still move, pass or shoot. A clean challenge to an exposed ball can still win it. Release Control to return to normal dribbling. Close Control takes priority over Sprint when both are held. Sprint pushes the ball further ahead. Without possession, the same control is defensive jockeying. The stamina bar is below the selected player's name; shot or pass power appears only while charging.

Keyboard navigation works with Tab/Shift+Tab and Enter/Space on semantic menu buttons. The controller maps gameplay actions; menus remain keyboard, pointer or touch driven. Press a controller button after connecting so the browser exposes it. Standard mappings are required. Disconnect clears controller state. Axis magnitude is preserved outside the dead zone. Physical controllers and browser-specific mappings need additional testing.

Hold Shoot while in possession to fill the central **SHOT POWER** bar. Its percentage reflects the actual shot charge, changing from green through amber to coral as strength increases. In open play, release to shoot; a full charge still fires automatically after approximately 0.95 seconds. The meter sits centrally in sideline views and near the top in Third person, updates each rendered frame, and clears on release, lost possession or opening a menu. Keyboard, controller and touch use the same meter. A pitch guide and target marker show the intended shooting direction and target from the same base aim and power calculation used at release. Adjust the movement/aim input while charging to change the target. Shooting ability, pressure, movement and body orientation still affect dispersion; the guide does not guarantee the ball's final path or a goal.

**Manual restarts:** free kicks, throw-ins, goal kicks, corners and penalties awarded to your team wait for you after a short positioning sequence. Release any action held at the whistle, then aim with WASD/arrows or the movement stick. Direction is retained when you let go of the stick. The taker stays beside the stationary ball. Hold/release Pass or Through to choose power, or Shoot to strike; Loft sends a cross. For throw-ins, Pass is a shorter throw, Shoot a long throw, and Loft an aerial throw. Full power waits for release; there is no automatic human restart timer. A pitch guide shows direction and target. Penalties at either end automatically use a view behind the taker, then return to your saved camera after the kick. Move the target left/right and up/down with WASD/arrows or the movement stick, then hold and release Shoot. Power changes shot speed independently of target height. Letting go of the stick retains the aim. The penalty guide appears before charging, and maximum power never takes the kick automatically. Pause, focus loss, cancelled touch input and stale/disconnected network input cancel a charge without taking the restart. AI restarts, including AI penalties, retain their automatic sequence.

**Aerial play:** tap Shoot shortly before a loose airborne ball reaches your selected player. A higher ball starts a jumping header; a lower one starts a volley. Contact only succeeds during the brief action window and within physical reach. Early/late attempts miss; holding Shoot does not repeatedly attempt headers. Aim with movement when pressing. The central hint indicates an aerial opportunity. Clear sends a headed or kicked clearance upfield; it is distinct from an aimed shot. AI opponents use the same contact rules. Aim corners into the box and use Loft for an aerial delivery, and offside still applies to involvement in non-exempt deliveries.

**Special shots:** hold Z / RB / R1 with Shoot for a slower, more precise curling finesse shot. Hold X / LT / L2 with Shoot for a chip over an advanced keeper; more power lifts the arc. The chip remains vulnerable to a keeper who stays near the goal, and long chips can fall short. Touch players choose Normal, Finesse or Chip in the selector beside Shoot. The power bar names the selected shot; the airborne guide uses the same flight integration as live ball physics and keeper forecasts, stopping at the goal plane or first landing. Dispersion and contacts are excluded from the guide. Change the modifier while charging to change style. If both modifiers are held, Chip wins. Releasing Shoot and the modifier together retains the last charged style.

Hold Pass or Through while in possession to fill **PASS POWER** or **THROUGH BALL** on the same meter. Aim with the movement control, then release. A quick tap retains a light pass; more charge adds pace and favours longer options, with extra forward lead for a through ball. Both automatically release at their 0.75-second cap in open play; manual restarts wait for release. The intended receiver and target guide update with aim, type and power. Losing possession, changing the selected player or entering a menu clears the visible charge; a held button must be released before another capped pass can begin. Assistance and accuracy still matter, so the guide is an intention rather than a guaranteed completion.

Touch captures the movement pointer separately from action pointers. Movement continues while charging a shot or pass. The two main action buttons change from Pass/Shoot to Tackle/Switch when the opponent has possession. The separate Slide button replaces the former standalone Tackle button; standing tackles remain on the contextual Tackle button, keyboard E and controller RB / R1. A slide commits the player to a short lunge and recovery, so time the challenge before pressing F, X / Square while defending, or Slide. Contact with an opponent before the ball can concede a foul or penalty. Buttons support independent fingers, safe-area margins and cancelled pointers. Landscape play is intended; portrait receives an orientation prompt. Browser zoom/gesture restrictions vary by device, and OS-reserved gestures cannot be disabled universally.

**Practice** on the title screen opens two offline drills. **Shooting & aim** teaches shot charge, movement aim and the target guide against a live goalkeeper. **Slide tackle timing** asks you to win the ball before contacting an approaching carrier. Use the same controls as an exhibition. Attempts count actual shots or slides; successes count goals or clean ball-first slide contacts. Feedback explains the outcome and automatically resets the setup. Release action buttons before the next attempt. **Restart drill** clears the counters, and **Exit practice** returns to the title. Practice does not create an exhibition result, enter an online room or trigger a goal replay.

An automatic goal replay shows up to the last three seconds in at most 2.6 seconds, within the existing three-second goal break. Press **Skip replay**, Space, Esc, or controller A / Cross or B / Circle to return to the current match view. The replay uses a temporary Telebroadcast view and restores your selected camera afterwards. It never pauses the live simulation, changes the score or delays the next kick-off; it ends immediately if live play leaves the goal phase. Gameplay actions are neutral during playback and the scoreboard continues to show the live score.

Losing focus clears device input. Offline play opens pause; online authority continues, and input becomes neutral after 250 ms without an update. A tab returning from the background receives current server state. Offline pause, restart and results are available from the match menu. Camera & settings is available during a match. Quit game opens a confirmation; cancelling preserves the match, and confirming leaves any online room, clears gameplay input, stops rendering and suspends audio. The browser tab remains open on an ended-session screen with an option to return to the title screen. Online pause only opens a menu and clears local input.

The Settings menu offers Broadcast, Telebroadcast and Third person. Height and zoom adjust all three views; in Third person they change the follow camera's elevation and distance. Field of view (60–100 degrees) adjusts Third person. Previously saved First person preferences automatically migrate to Third person. Camera preferences, graphics, shadows, reduced camera motion, touch display and separate audio levels are stored locally. Master volume zero mutes everything. Audio begins after interaction. There is no flashing goal strobe, and no information depends solely on audio. Screen-reader access covers menus and text HUD, but the spatial football action itself is not fully screen-reader accessible. Keyboard/controller bindings do not yet offer arbitrary remapping.

**Customize touch controls:** open Settings → Customize touch controls. Drag any action button, the movement joystick or the shot selector. Select a control and use Size (80–150%) to resize it; arrow keys can move the selected control. Save applies the layout on this browser. Cancel discards edits, and Reset prepares the default layout for saving. Overlap outlines prevent saving accidentally stacked controls. Landscape and portrait layouts are stored separately. Controls stay within the viewport; if shrinking the viewport would overlap saved controls, the safe default is displayed without erasing the saved layout.

**Automatic graphics:** enabled by default. The chosen graphics quality is the maximum. Sustained slow active-play frames first reduce detail/shadows and then render resolution; sustained recovery restores them gradually. Pauses, menus, background tabs and single long gaps do not drive the adjustment. Turn the option off to use fixed graphics. All 22 players and the simulation remain unchanged. These controls improve GPU workload, without guaranteeing a particular frame rate on every device.

### Match audio

Settings → Master controls all sound; Effects controls contact, referee and menu cues; Crowd controls ambience, anticipation and reactions. Values are saved locally, and Master zero is respected from the first gesture. Audio starts on a click/tap/key interaction, lowers behind match menus, suspends in hidden tabs and on Quit, and resumes on the next interaction. No new gameplay buttons are required.


### First-time finishing and one-twos

Tap Shoot up to 0.4 seconds before an incoming ground pass reaches your selected player. The player shoots at physical contact, keeping the chosen Normal, Finesse or Chip style. A short tap uses moderate power; holding through arrival adds power within the short buffer. No extra touch or settling delay is required. First-time input can also take control of the intended receiver during the passer's follow-through. Switching, another action, losing possession, expiry, pause or interrupted input cancels it; offside still applies.

Clean, balanced shots inside the penalty area have 40% less random dispersion. Pressure, sprinting, poor body orientation and long range still affect accuracy. Settings → Close-range shooting assist is on by default and saved on this device. It gently helps a balanced close shot towards a clear corner if no side is selected, or further towards the chosen side; full directional aim, shot style and power remain yours. Turn it off for the original directional target. The guide and authoritative shot share the same aim. Manual restarts and penalties retain their existing aiming rules.

Press R, use LB / L1 + A / Cross, or hold touch Control and tap Pass for a one-two. Your player passes and runs forward; control transfers to the receiver. On touch, Pass reads “1–2” while Control is held in possession. Aim towards the runner and use Pass or Through for the return; it is never automatic. The runner tries to hold an onside position until release. Runs wait up to five seconds for a return pass, then allow up to three seconds for its flight. They end on a turnover, dead ball, different recipient, shot, clearance or completed return. Receiving possession transfers control immediately, even during the original passer’s follow-through. LB alone switches on release while attacking; defensive switching remains on press. Existing custom touch layouts are preserved.


**Penalty Practice:** choose Practice → Penalty Practice from the title menu. Aim and shoot against the keeper; each real shot counts as an attempt and each goal as a success. Goals, saves and misses show feedback, then reset the spot and aim for another kick. Release held actions before the next attempt. Restart clears the counters; Exit returns to the title menu.
